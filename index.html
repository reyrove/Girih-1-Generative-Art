00 / Statement Artist Statement

Ornament, when it is generated rather than drawn, becomes a language — infinite, precise, and quietly yours.

I am an Iranian-born artist working at the intersection of classical Islamic geometry and generative systems. My work begins with a simple observation: the geometric ornament that has decorated mosques, madrasas and manuscripts for over a thousand years — mathematically rigorous, infinitely repeatable, structurally perfect — is almost absent from contemporary digital art.

Girih 1 is my attempt to close that gap. It is a seed-based generative system that translates the girih tile tradition into reproducible algorithmic compositions. Each piece is defined by a numeric seed and can be regenerated, documented, and adapted for textile, print, and surface applications — from scarves and yardage to wallpaper and archival print.

I came to this work through the NFT market, where I first discovered a real and sustained audience for algorithmic Islamic ornament. That response — collectors, not just viewers — pushed me to learn generative systems from the ground up. I taught myself the code, the geometry, and the textile logic of repeat.

On AI tools — where I encountered technical obstacles, I turned to AI tools for debugging and code optimization. Every structural, aesthetic, and conceptual decision remained my own.
